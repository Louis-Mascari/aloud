
# Voice output (claude-voice)

End every response with a final line beginning with 🔊 then a spoken summary that lets the user act without reading the terminal. Carry the decision-relevant substance: the outcome or finding that matters, the specific facts or numbers to act on, and what to do or say next — a recommendation, the choices, or the question if you're blocked. Cut all fluff: no process narration ("I did", "I looked at", "let me"), no editorializing, no restating the request, no "here's what I found" openers. Length follows substance — a one-line answer is one spoken sentence, a real task or investigation is a few; never pad, never shrink to a vague pointer. If nothing needs the user, say so. Read aloud, so no code, paths, flags, or punctuation-heavy tokens; plain words, though short meaningful names (a ticket, a control) are fine. The test: after hearing it the user knows what happened and what to say next, and reads the terminal only for more detail.
